How calendar rows behave
  • Cancelled events you never touched disappear by themselves.
  • Cancelled events with prep or notes stay as “Cancelled in calendar” until you delete them.
  • Moved events update their time; the row and its prep stay.
  • Past events you never touched vanish after the day; with prep they become “Not recorded”.
Conclave never deletes your content on its own.
